# Clip Queue, Numbering & Export Flow

- Title decoupling: `cleanClipTitle` strips leading numbers so ordinal numbering stays independent of clip title.
- Custom starting index: `clipStartIndex` (`localStorage("clipgen_start_index")`) shifts displayed list numbers, timeline badges, and exported filenames (`clip_start_index` in Rust).
- Insert at any number: `VisorPanel.tsx` `#clip-position-input` (`clipNumberInput`) passes `insertAtNumber` to `App.tsx` `handleAddClip`, splicing into `savedClips` and reindexing subsequent clips. Existing clips can also jump to any number by clicking their `#displayNum` badge in `ClipQueuePanel.tsx` (`handleMoveClipToNumber`).
- Scope visibility: `App.tsx` passes `currentVideoUrl={metadata?.webpage_url ?? null}` (without `|| url`) and sets `clipsLoaded` only after `load_persistent_clips` succeeds. When `!hasLoadedVideo`, `ClipQueuePanel` stays in `"all"` mode with a global queue banner; when loaded, it always renders `Video Actual` / `Todos en cola` tabs (`onSelectAll` operates on `visibleClips`).
- Export options: `handleExportQualityPresetChange` updates both global `options.quality_preset` and queued clips; `buildBatchRequests` groups by video and applies `marginBefore`/`marginAfter` + `filenameFormat` (`clean` vs `full`).
