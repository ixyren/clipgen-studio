# Timeline & Video Player Architecture

## 3-Zone NLE Timeline (`src/components/VisorPanel.tsx`)
- Zone A (`h-6`): Ruler ticks (down to 0.2s/0.5s sub-second intervals when zoomed in tightly), buffered/loaded video bars (`bufferedRanges` cyan indicator in header, minimap, and Zone A from `v.buffered` + `Hls.Events.FRAG_BUFFERED`), timecodes, amber chapter markers, immediate playhead scrub.
- Zone B (`h-9`): Saved Clips lane (`selectedTimelineClipId`) with always-reachable left/right trim handles, non-blocking alignment guides, and click-to-select.
- Zone C (`h-9`): Active Selection `[ I - O ]` lane with `I`/`O` handles and draggable middle `range-move` bar; becomes `opacity-20 pointer-events-none` when holding `Alt` (`isAltPressed`).
- Zoom range: presets `[1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500]` with dynamic `maxZoom = Math.max(500, Math.min(2500, Math.ceil(duration / 2)))`.
- In/Out marking (`markInAtCurrent` `[ I ]`, `markOutAtCurrent` `[ O ]`): marking `I` at/past `activeEnd - 0.1` shifts the opposite bound cleanly by `+15s` (clamped to `[0, duration]`), and marking `O` at/before `activeStart + 0.1` shifts `activeStart` by `-15s` instead of clamping against the old bound.
- Auto-follow Center Playhead (**Fijar al Centro**, `followPlayhead`): `handleToggleFollowPlayhead` locks the playhead to the center (`computeCenteredViewStart`, auto-zooming to `4x` from `1x` on long videos), updating live at 60fps during playback, on `seekTo`, during `playhead` drag, and anchoring `handleZoomChange` at `0.5`; manual pan (`handlePan`, `handleFitSelection`, `handleMinimapPointerDown`) unlocks it cleanly.
- Protruding Playhead Handle: rendered as an outer sibling overlay (`inset-x-[1px] z-40`) inside `<div className="relative pt-3.5">` above `timelineRef` (`overflow-hidden`), giving a `16px x 20px` draggable grab tab `-2px` above Zone A so users can grab and scrub the playhead without colliding with clips or trim handles.
- Smooth Velocity-Responsive Scrubbing & Left-Shift Slow-Motion: `beginTimelineDrag(mode, initialTime, startClientX)` uses relative pointer delta integration (`scrubPhysicsRef`, `computeScrubGain`) combined with a 60fps `requestAnimationFrame` exponential damping loop (`1 - Math.exp(-dt * lambda)`), committing `physics.displayTime` on release. Holding Left Shift (`e.code === "ShiftLeft"`, `isLeftShiftPressedRef`) engages `0.08x–0.28x` fine slow-motion scrubbing with `modeHint` displayed on the floating `<canvas>` `trimPreview` tooltip; normal playhead drag scales smoothly (`0.55x–1.0x` micro-precision to `1.0x–2.35x` fast acceleration). Snapping stays active via separate `rawTargetTime` vs snapped `targetTime`.
- Anti-hang during drags: serial non-aborting seek queue (`queuedSeekTimeRef`, `flushQueuedSeek`, 85ms cooldown waiting on `v.seeking`/`onSeeked`), stale `handleTimeUpdate` guard during drag, hidden dark play/loading overlays while dragging, and floating `<canvas>` mini-frame preview tooltip (`trimPreview`, `drawTrimPreviewFrame`).
- Auto-stop at clip end (**Parar al fin**, `stopAtClipEnd`): mutually exclusive toggle beside **Bucle** (`handleToggleStopAtEnd` / `handleToggleLoop`). High-precision `checkStopAtClipEnd` runs via both `handleTimeUpdate` and a `requestAnimationFrame` loop while playing, pausing `<video>` + `<audio>` cleanly right at `activeEnd`, keeping deliberate seeks past `activeEnd + 0.5s` playing, and rewinding back to `activeStart` in `togglePlay` when resuming from `activeEnd`.

## Viewport Loading Progress (`src-tauri/src/ytdlp.rs`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/components/VisorPanel.tsx`)
- `fetch_video_info(app, url)` emits `video-load-progress` milestones (`8%` yt-dlp -> `45%` formats/chapters -> `58%` audio probes -> `70%` preview streams -> `80%` starting player).
- `src/App.tsx` forwards `isAnalyzing`, `analyzeProgress`, and `analyzeMessage` into `VisorPanel`.
- `VisorPanel` unifies initial analysis (`0% -> 80%`) and stream attachment/HLS (`MANIFEST_PARSED`, `FRAG_LOADING`, `FRAG_LOADED`, `FRAG_BUFFERED`, `<video>` `onLoadedMetadata`/`onLoadedData`) into `80% -> 100%` (or standalone `0% -> 100%` during resolution switch / rebuffering), interpolated smoothly at 60fps inside a compact SVG circular progress ring (`showLoadOverlay`, `loadProgress`, `loadLabel`) right over the video viewport.

## Speed & Resolution Synchronization (`src/components/VisorPanel.tsx`)
- `syncMediaSettings()` enforces both `defaultPlaybackRate` and `playbackRate` from `playbackRateRef` plus `muted` across `<video>` and `<audio>` on load, metadata, `canplay`, `canplaythrough`, `playing`, `ratechange`, and `timeupdate` (drift > 0.25s resyncs audio).
- Separate `audioHlsRef` attachment effect handles dedicated direct or HLS audio tracks without rebuilding video HLS.
- `handleResolutionChange` sets `"Cambiando a ..."` on the viewport loader, saves `pendingResolutionRestoreTimeRef`, and preserves `wantPlayRef`.

## Anti-Stall Protection (`src/components/VisorPanel.tsx` & `src-tauri/src/ytdlp.rs`)
- Player: `hls.js` retry config + 1s interval watchdog triggers soft `hls.startLoad()` / 0.04s nudge after 3.2s stall, then silent same-quality reload via `reloadNonce` at exact `v.currentTime`.
- Rust `/stream/` proxy: `proxy_http_client()` uses 10s connect timeout, 20s pool idle timeout, 15s keepalive, 90s request timeout; `/stream/` chunk loop retries up to 3 times with 12s upstream/chunk timeouts from exact byte offset `cur` on network drops, timeouts, or 5xx/429 responses.
